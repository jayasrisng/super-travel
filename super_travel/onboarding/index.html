Ask us when we’re ready
01What does Wonder Travel actually do?

Wonder Travel brings everyone’s dates, budgets, and tastes into one shared plan. The trip-planning demos on this page are illustrative. On-demand discovery is live: search a destination or use your location to find nearby places to eat, explore, and experience from OpenStreetMap.

02Is this only for group trips?

Not at all. On-demand discovery helps you find things to do wherever you are, whether you’re solo or with friends. The group-planning concept makes it easier to get everyone aligned before a bigger trip.

03Does it book things without asking?

High-stakes bookings are reviewed by a human before moving forward. The product concept is built around clear choices and confirmation, not surprise bookings.

04How does the saved-video import work?

A future version could use an export you choose to provide to understand the kinds of places and activities you like. Wonder Travel stores only a taste profile, not the raw source data, and you can delete that profile any time.

05What happens to my email on this page?

The waitlist form currently saves your address only in this browser’s local storage as a demo. It is not sent to a company or server. You can clear it by clearing this site’s local storage.

06Are these prices live?

No. All prices shown are placeholders for discussion. Any recovery fee would apply only when value is recovered; no price or service is currently being offered here.
